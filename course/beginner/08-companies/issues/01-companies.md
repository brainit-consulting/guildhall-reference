# Companies

Many of my contacts work for the same businesses. I want to keep the businesses too.

## What it should do
- A Companies item in the menu, opening `/companies`: a table of name, website and phone.
- "Add company": name (required), website, phone and notes.
- A company page with every field, "Edit" and "Delete" (with "Delete <name>? This can't be undone.").
- With no companies yet: "No companies yet. Add your first."

## Done when
- [ ] I can add, view, edit and delete a company.
- [ ] Tests: integration tests for creating, updating and deleting a company; a Playwright test that adds one.
- [ ] Tried in a browser
