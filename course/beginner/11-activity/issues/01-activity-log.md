# The activity log

I need a record of every call, meeting and note, on the person, company or deal it was about.

## What it should do
- One list of activities for the whole Guildhall. Each has a kind (note, call, meeting, stage change), the text, who
  logged it, when, and which contact, company or deal it belongs to.
- "Log activity" on every contact, company and deal page: pick note, call or meeting, write the text, save.
- Each of those pages shows its activities as a timeline, newest first, with who and when ("Emile, 12 Oct 2026").

- [ ] I can log a call on a contact and see it on that contact's timeline.
- [ ] Tests: integration tests for logging and listing activities; a Playwright test that logs a note on a deal.
