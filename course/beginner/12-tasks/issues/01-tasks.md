# Tasks

I need to keep track of what I've promised to do, and for whom.

## What it should do
- A task has a title, a due date, done or not, and belongs to one contact, company or deal.
- "Add task" on every contact, company and deal page, and on a new `/tasks` page (where you pick what it belongs to).
- `/tasks` (a Tasks item in the menu): open tasks by due date, each linking to what it belongs to; a tick box to mark
  it done; a "Show done" switch.
- Each contact, company and deal page lists its open tasks.

## Done when
- [ ] I can add a task to a deal, see it on `/tasks` and on the deal, and tick it off.
- [ ] Tests: integration tests for adding, listing and completing tasks; a Playwright test that adds and ticks one.
- [ ] Tried in a browser
